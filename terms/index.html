Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create a Lokt account or place a stake. By using Lokt, you confirm that you are 18 or older.

How stakes work

On Lokt, you create a "stake" by:

  1. Picking a goal — a template ("Wake up," "Go to the gym," "Cook a meal") or a custom goal you write yourself;
  2. Setting a deadline;
  3. Choosing a stake amount between ₹20 and ₹5,000;
  4. Choosing a charity from Lokt's partner list, to receive your stake if you miss your goal; and
  5. Paying via Razorpay.

Once paid, your stake is active until it is resolved — either by successful proof, a missed window, or a cancellation as described below. There is no prize and no winnings on Lokt: the most you can ever receive back is the stake amount you paid in, and there is no betting or wagering between users.

Platform fee

You are charged the stake amount you choose — nothing is added at checkout. If a stake fails, Lokt retains 10% of it as a service fee (covering proof checks, payment processing and running the app) and the remaining 90% is donated to the charity you selected. The percentage is published in the app before you stake and may change for future stakes; the rate that applies to a stake is fixed when you create it. If you complete your goal, or cancel in time, Lokt retains nothing and the full amount is refunded.

Proof rules and review

Each goal has a proof window: it opens before your deadline (15 minutes for puzzle-style goals, up to a few hours for photo goals depending on the goal) and closes 15 minutes after your deadline. All window times are measured by Lokt's server clock, not your device's clock.

  • Puzzle goals (such as "Wake up") require you to solve a short in-app puzzle within the window.
  • Photo goals (such as "Go to the gym" or "Cook a meal") require you to submit a photo within the window. Photos are reviewed by the Lokt team; if a photo isn't reviewed within 12 hours of submission, it is automatically approved in your favour.

If valid proof isn't submitted and approved before the window closes, the goal is treated as missed.

Cancellation

You may cancel an active stake up to 4 hours before its proof window opens, for a full refund of the stake and fee. Once that cutoff has passed, a stake cannot be cancelled. If you create a stake but don't complete payment, it is automatically cancelled after 30 minutes and you are not charged.

Refunds

If you complete your goal and your proof is approved, the full amount you paid is refunded to your original payment method. The same applies if you cancel a stake within the cancellation window. Refunds are issued via Razorpay; banks typically take 5–7 working days to show them. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for full details.

Donations are final

If a stake fails — no valid proof by the time the window closes, or a photo is rejected — the stake amount is donated to the charity you chose when you created the stake. This donation is final once made: Lokt does not reverse, refund, or return a failed stake to you. Lokt never keeps a failed stake for itself.
